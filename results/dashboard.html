⚑Verdict

Verdict. imagined corrections change outcomes (+4.2pp vs doing nothing), but they are indistinguishable from an equal-budget random perturbation (-1.1pp, p = 0.61) — the model's direction is not where the value is. Training on the corrections (ft_all 57.0%) matches a replay-only control (ft_replay 58.0%), and concentrating corrections where the decision-point metric looked best is actively harmful (ft_early 45.0% vs smolvla_libero 61.0%). Offline value at a state does not tell you what to teach there.

⚑结论

结论。 想象纠错确实改变结果（相对不纠正 +4.2pp），但与等预算随机扰动无法区分（-1.1pp，p = 0.61）——模型的方向不是价值所在。用纠错训练（ft_all 57.0%）与仅回放对照（ft_replay 58.0%）持平，而把纠错集中到决策指标最看好的位置反而有害（ft_early 45.0% vs smolvla_libero 61.0%）。状态上的离线价值并不告诉你在该状态该教什么。
